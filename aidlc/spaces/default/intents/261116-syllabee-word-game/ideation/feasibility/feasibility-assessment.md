# Feasibility Assessment: Syllabee

## Summary

Syllabee is feasible as scoped. The product is small (one game page, one shared passcode, no accounts), the team has already settled its delivery approach, no regulatory requirements apply (Q2), there is no deadline (Q4), and no organisational blockers exist (Q7). The open points are practical: hosting must be set up and its cost kept low, and the word list must be supplied.

## Viability by Area

| Area | Assessment | Basis |
|------|------------|-------|
| Scope | Viable. Small, well-bounded, three ordered releases (v0.1, v0.2, v0.3) | intent-statement.md, Initial Scope Signal |
| Technical | Viable. No outside systems to connect to; the team's approach is already chosen | Q1 |
| Regulatory | None apply; no personal data is collected | Q2, Q1 |

| Organisation | No blockers identified | Q7 |

## Main Concerns

- The "free allowance" budget may not match what the hosting provider offers today (hypothesis, to verify before the first release goes live).
- The word list does not exist yet; the game cannot be played without it (v0.2 depends on it).
- The hosting account setup is a human-only step, so v0.1 going live waits on it.

## Recommendation

Proceed. Verify hosting cost early and agree a date for the word list before the playable release (v0.2).

## Assumptions & Open Questions

- Hypothesis: the hosting provider offers an allowance that keeps staging and production at or near zero cost. Not yet verified.
